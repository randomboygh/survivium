Survivium is open source and sold by its author. Different parts have different licences:

| Code: `src/`, `tools/`, `tests/`, `shaders/`, scripts and the workflows | GNU GPL v3.0 only | [LICENSE](LICENSE) |
| Assets and content data: `assets/` (except the font), `content/`, `icon.png`, the sound and picture files | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | [LICENSE-ASSETS](LICENSE-ASSETS) |
| Font `assets/fonts/PressStart2P.ttf` | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | [assets/fonts/OFL.txt](assets/fonts/OFL.txt) |
| Third-party tools and libraries | their own licences | [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md) |

In short:
- You may read, run, change and share the code under the GPL: a copy you distribute must be open under the
  same licence. Selling a copy is allowed by the GPL, but only with its source and without closing it.
- You may use the pictures and sounds, with credit, for non-commercial purposes only, and what you make from
  them must carry the same licence. The author, who owns them, may sell the game with them.
- Mods (data in your own mod folder) are yours: you choose their licence. A mod that copies the game's
  own assets stays under CC BY-NC-SA for those assets.
- The game's name and logo are not covered by any of these licences.

## Contributions

By sending a pull request you confirm the work is yours (or you may share it) and you license it under the
licence of the part it changes, and you also grant the project's author the right to include it in the
author's own commercial builds under other terms (Steam, Google Play and other stores). Without this the
change cannot be taken.
